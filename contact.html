Travel between these three heritage centers is very convenient. Rajgir and Nalanda are just 15 kilometers apart (a 25-minute drive). Bodh Gaya to Rajgir is approximately 70 kilometers via smooth four-lane state highways, taking under 2 hours by private cab or government tourist coaches.
